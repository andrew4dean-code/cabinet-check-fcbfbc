Read before calling a piece missing

  • Drawn twice, ordered once: W3636B, WF642, and BBC42/45-PO-L each show on both the sink wall and range wall drawings. Each is one piece.
  • Same code, two rooms: WF330 is used at the kitchen corner and again at the basement vanity.
  • Kitchen and bath shipped as one order. An extra piece in the kitchen may belong to a bathroom. Type its code above to see where it goes.
  • Fridge sides: each side has two pieces, a 1½" REF96 panel plus a ¼" PQTR skin on its outer face. That is 4 pieces total, 39½" across with the 36" opening.
  • Blind corner: BBC42/45-PO-L is a 39" box set 5½" off the sink wall. The gap behind it is on purpose.
  • Toe kick: the drawings say Toekick: Yes and draw 8 runs but list no toe kick part. Job photos show raw toe space. Ask the supplier whether finished toe kick was included.
  • Island back: PHLF is the panel drawn on the back of the island. The back was still raw in the Nov 20 photo.
  • Crown: drawn with a joint at 96", so it takes at least 2 lengths. The run is about 189" with 3 mitered corners, and 2 lengths give only 192". Plan on a third length.
  • REF96 fridge panels come 96" tall and are drawn to about 90", so they get cut on site.
  • Island location has no dimension on the drawings. Its position here is scaled from a plan marked No Scale.
  • Appliances are shown as drawn and are not part of the cabinet order. Confirm the final fridge fits the 36" opening.
  • Countertop, sink, faucet, floor, wall color, and pulls are placeholders.
